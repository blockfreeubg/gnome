GNOME brand assets, design v2 (2026-11-12; docs/DESIGN.md §2)

All files in this directory are original work made for GNOME and are distributed under GNOME's own license
(AGPL-3.0-only). Nothing here is copied from another product or site.

gnome-mark-mono.svg            The monochrome GNOME mark (fill="currentColor", viewBox 0 0 382 499 like
                               ../brand/gnome-mark.png). Derived from that transparent cutout of ../logo.png: the
                               cutout's alpha was smoothed into a silhouette, a few cuts were added (the shadow under
                               the hat brim, a ring and shadow around the nose, the beard line, the mushroom cap) and
                               the result was traced into one path. The original logo.png and ../brand/ are unchanged.
gnome-mark-mono-white-*.png/.webp   The mark rasterized in #f5f5f5 (dark theme), 48/96/192/384 px tall.
gnome-mark-mono-ink-*.png/.webp     The mark rasterized in #111111 (light theme), 48/96/192/384 px tall.
favicon.svg, favicon.ico, favicon-32.png, favicon-64.png, apple-touch-icon.png, icon-192.png, icon-512.png,
icon-maskable-512.png          App icons: the white mark on a #0a0a0a tile (the maskable icon keeps the mark inside
                               the 80% safe zone).

wallpaper/grove-dark.avif/.webp     "Grove", the default Home wallpaper (2560x1440): a moonlit spruce clearing in the
wallpaper/grove-light.avif/.webp    mist, and the same scene at a misty dawn for the light theme. Generated
                                    procedurally (seeded canvas drawing of layered spruce silhouettes, mist bands, a
                                    moon glow and a few fireflies, plus a fine film grain), no photographs involved.
